# Frontend interview loops by company (~3 YOE / SDE2, India + global), Nov 2025 – Nov 2026

Method note: 11 web searches and 5 page fetches (2 failed: Medium 403, GreatFrontEnd 429). Most evidence is self-reported candidate posts (Medium, LeetCode Discuss, Glassdoor, GFG) or prep-site guides (SpaceComplexity, intervue.io, Prepfully, FrontendLead). Those prep guides are aggregators, not primary sources. Many Medium posts could not be opened, so details come from search snippets. Where a date could not be confirmed, the row says "date unverified". No company published an official frontend-specific loop description that I could find.

Confidence key: **H** = 3+ independent sources that agree, at least one from 2026; **M** = 2 sources, or 1 recent detailed source; **L** = 1 thin, old or aggregator-only source; **None** = no frontend data found.

## Which companies still have a dedicated DSA round for frontend roles, and at what difficulty?

### Takeaway
Big Tech (Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft) and the "product-MNC" tier (Atlassian, Uber, Adobe, Intuit, Walmart, PhonePe, Zeta, Meesho, Flipkart's PS/DS rounds) still run at least one separate DSA round for frontend roles, mostly at **medium** LeetCode level. Google is the strictest: it uses the same bar as backend SWE, with two onsite DSA rounds. Easy-to-medium array, string and two-pointer questions are typical at Walmart, PhonePe UI and Zeta.

## Which companies replaced DSA with JS problem-solving / machine coding?

### Takeaway
Most Indian consumer startups and fintechs run **no LeetCode-style DSA round** for frontend SDE2. They test through **machine coding plus JS fundamentals and polyfills**, often with a take-home: Zepto, CRED, Groww, Swiggy (2026), Razorpay (LLD + React MC), BrowserStack, Media.net (plain-JS problem solving), Sprinklr senior FE, and Publicis Sapient (pure JS/React depth). Even companies that keep DSA (Flipkart, PhonePe, Walmart, Meta, Google) add a separate JS/machine-coding round. Machine coding is close to universal.

## Which emphasize frontend system design at ~3 YOE?

### Takeaway
A dedicated FE system design round at around 3 YOE is reliably reported at **Google, Meta, Amazon (SDE2), Uber, Meesho and Razorpay**, and sometimes at Flipkart. At **Atlassian it depends on levelling**: P30 (1–4 YOE) has none and P40 does. Walmart, Adobe and Intuit tend to add FE/web system design at senior (SDE-3 / CS-II) levels. Startups like Zepto, Groww, CRED and BrowserStack test performance knowledge inside JS/React rounds instead of running a formal SD round.

### Cited Findings
- Meta UI Design round: component architecture (autocomplete, infinite scroll) or FE system design (collaborative editor, dashboard); RADIO framework — [SpaceComplexity Meta](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/meta-frontend-engineer-interview)
- Google: UI-focused system design (typeahead, feed, video player) — [SpaceComplexity Google](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/google-frontend-engineer-interview)
- Amazon: separate FE system design round — [FE Interview Handbook Amazon](https://www.frontendinterviewhandbook.com/companies/amazon-front-end-interview-questions)
- Uber: 1–2 system design rounds in the FE onsite — [Prepfully Uber](https://prepfully.com/interview-guides/uber-frontend-engineer-interview-process); Bengaluru report of DSA + MC + SD + managerial — [Glassdoor Uber FE](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Uber-Frontend-Engineer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE575263.0,4_KO5,22.htm)
- Atlassian: P30 has no SD, P40 adds it, and FE roles get FE SD — [intervue.io](https://www.intervue.io/blog/atlassian-interview-experience-india); example "design a JIRA board" — [FrontendLead](https://frontendlead.com/discuss/t/atlassian-interview-experience/3511)

### Inferences
- At ~3 YOE, prepare at least one FE SD framework (RADIO) with classic prompts: autocomplete/typeahead, infinite feed, dashboard, chat or collaborative editor, JIRA/Kanban board. Most Tier-A/B companies and several India startups at SDE2 ask one.
- Levelling matters. At Atlassian, Walmart and Adobe, being levelled one step up adds an SD round.

### Gaps
- Microsoft, Zomato, Swiggy, PhonePe and Intuit at SDE2: no clear evidence either way of a dedicated FE SD round.
- Interview-loop changes after Nov 2025 (beyond Meta's AI-assisted round) were not found for most companies.
